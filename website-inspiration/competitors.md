## What the Best Sites Have in Common
1. Big, beautiful hero photos of styled porches
2. 3 clear pricing tiers (small, medium, large)
3. Simple online ordering / booking form
4. Before & after photos
5. "How it works" section (order → we deliver → we style → you enjoy)
6. Service area map or zip code list
7. Seasonal urgency ("book now, spots are limited")

## Key Takeaway for Our Site
Keep it simple. A one-page site with: hero image, 3 packages, a booking form, and an "about us" section is all we need to start. We can use Squarespace, Wix, or Carrd to build it fast and cheap.
