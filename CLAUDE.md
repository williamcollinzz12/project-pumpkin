# Chicago Pumpkin Authority

## What This Is
**Chicago Pumpkin Authority** is a seasonal pumpkin porch delivery and design business serving wealthy families in Lincoln Park, Chicago (near Kingston Mines). We deliver, arrange, and style pumpkins on customers' front porches so they get a beautiful fall display without doing any work.

- **Website**: chicagopumpkinauthority.com (to be built)
- **Instagram**: @chicagopumpkinauthority
- **Facebook**: Chicago Pumpkin Authority

## The Team
- 4 guys splitting costs and labor equally
- We have a car for deliveries
- Costco membership for sourcing pumpkins
- Based in Lincoln Park, Chicago

## Business Model
- **Order-first, buy-second**: We only purchase pumpkins AFTER a customer places an order. This means zero inventory risk — we never get stuck with unsold pumpkins.
- **Revenue**: Pumpkin arrangement packages in the $449–$849 range
- **Sourcing**: "Clybourn Loop" — Costco ($6.99 heirlooms, $7.99 jumbos), Aldi ($3.95 mediums), Home Depot ($7.98 hay bales), Jewel-Osco ($6.99 white pumpkins). Full sourcing research in `business-plan/sourcing-research.md`
- **Cost per Signature package**: ~$170 for 38 units → sells for $649 → 74% margin
- **Marketing**: Facebook/Instagram ads targeting affluent Lincoln Park families
- **Season**: September through November (peak: October)

## Key Advantage
Most Chicago competitors focus on the North Shore suburbs. Lincoln Park proper (urban, walkable, dense with wealthy young families) appears underserved. Our mid-range pricing ($449–$849) undercuts competitors while still hitting 70–82% gross margins thanks to the Costco/Aldi sourcing strategy. Heirlooms that cost $15–$25 at boutique nurseries are $6.99 at Costco — that's our secret weapon.
