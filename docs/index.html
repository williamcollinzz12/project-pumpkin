About Us

Four guys. One neighborhood. A lot of pumpkins.

Born in Lincoln Park

We're four friends who live in Lincoln Park and noticed something: the best porch pumpkin services all focus on the North Shore suburbs. Meanwhile, our neighborhood — packed with beautiful brownstones and young families who love fall — had nothing.

So we started Chicago Pumpkin Authority. We source premium heirloom pumpkins, deliver them to your door, and hand-style your porch into something worth stopping for. You don't lift a finger.

Why we're different

We only buy pumpkins after you order, which means zero waste and the freshest possible product. We focus exclusively on Lincoln Park, so we know the porches, the vibes, and the neighborhood. And our pricing sits well below the competition while delivering the same premium look.
