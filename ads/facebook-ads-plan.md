# Chicago Pumpkin Authority — Facebook & Instagram Ads Plan

## Budget
Start with **$500/month** split 4 ways ($125 each). Scale up to $1,000 if orders are coming in.

## Target Audience
- **Location**: Lincoln Park, Lakeview, Old Town, Gold Coast, Bucktown, Wicker Park (Chicago zip codes: 60614, 60657, 60610, 60611, 60622, 60647)
- **Age**: 28–55
- **Household income**: Top 25% (Facebook allows income targeting)
- **Interests**: Home decor, fall activities, Halloween, Pottery Barn, Crate & Barrel, real estate, parenting
- **Homeowners** (Facebook has this filter)

## Ad Types to Run
1. **Lead Form Ad** — "Book Your Porch Pumpkin Delivery" with a simple form (name, address, package choice). These have the lowest cost per lead (~$8–$20 per interested person).
2. **Image/Carousel Ad** — Show off beautiful porch arrangements. Link to website.
3. **Video Ad** — 15-second time-lapse of an arrangement being set up. These get the most engagement.

## Expected Results
- Average cost per lead on Facebook: ~$19
- With $500/month budget: ~26 leads
- If 30-40% convert to orders: **8–10 orders from ads alone**
- At $549 avg order: **$4,400–$5,500 in revenue from $500 in ads**

## Ad Copy Ideas

### Ad 1: "The Easy Button"
"Lincoln Park — fall is almost here. Skip the pumpkin patch chaos. We deliver and style gorgeous pumpkin displays right on your porch. Starting at $399. Book now — spots fill fast."

### Ad 2: "The Neighbor Envy"
"Your neighbors will ask who did your porch. Premium pumpkin arrangements delivered and styled in Lincoln Park. 3 packages to choose from. Limited spots for October."

### Ad 3: "The Gift Angle"
"Surprise someone with the most Instagrammable porch in Lincoln Park. Pumpkin delivery + styling. The perfect fall gift."

## Timing
- Start ads: **Early September**
- Peak spending: **October 1–20** (this is when demand is hottest)
- Wind down: **Late October** (offer Thanksgiving arrangements to extend the season)

## Important
- Always use real photos of your work (even if it's a friend's porch as a demo first)
- Respond to every lead within 1 hour — fast response = more conversions
- Track every lead in a shared Google Sheet so all 4 of you can see what's coming in
