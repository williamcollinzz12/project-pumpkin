# Business Name Ideas

## Top Picks
1. **LP Pumpkins** — "LP" for Lincoln Park. Short, local, easy to remember.
2. **Porch & Gourd** — Sounds upscale, plays on "porch and garden."
3. **The Pumpkin Drop** — Fun, action-oriented, implies delivery.
4. **Stoop Pumpkins** — Chicago has stoops. Feels local and specific.
5. **Gourd Season** — Simple, seasonal, works as a brand.

## More Options
6. **Park Pumpkins** — Nod to Lincoln Park.
7. **Curb Appeal Pumpkins** — Describes exactly what you do.
8. **Front Porch Co.** — Clean, could expand beyond just pumpkins later.
9. **The Gourd Guys** — Fun, memorable, fits a crew of 4 dudes.
10. **Pumpkin Porters** — "Porters" = people who carry/deliver things.

## What Makes a Good Name for This Business
- Easy to spell and Google
- Works as an Instagram handle (@lpumpkins, @thepumpkindrop, etc.)
- Sounds premium but approachable (not too corporate)
- Ideally hints at either the location (Lincoln Park) or the service (delivery/porch)
