## Important Notes

1. **No inventory risk**: We only buy pumpkins after the order is confirmed and paid. We never lose money on unsold stock.

2. **Mini pumpkins are your margin friend**: A bag of ~10 mini pumpkins costs $6 but looks great filling in gaps. Use them generously — they're cheap and add visual volume.

3. **Payment upfront**: Always collect payment before purchasing and delivering. Venmo/Zelle to start, Stripe once the website is live.

4. **Delivery windows**: Batch deliveries by neighborhood to save gas. Try to do 3–5 deliveries per run.

5. **Sourcing tip**: Use the "Clybourn Loop" — Costco → Aldi → Home Depot, all within a few blocks. Hit them early October for best heirloom selection. See [sourcing-research.md](sourcing-research.md) for the full route and backup stores.

6. **Time commitment**: Each delivery + arrangement takes roughly 30–60 minutes. With 4 people, split into 2 teams of 2.
