## The "Clybourn Loop" — Our Sourcing Route

The most efficient way to buy everything in one trip:

1. **Start at Costco** (2746 N Clybourn) — heirlooms, jumbos, minis
2. **Walk next door to Aldi** (2600 N Clybourn) — medium orange pumpkins
3. **Drive to Home Depot** (1232 W North Ave) — hay bales
4. Optional: **Jewel-Osco** for white ghost pumpkins if Costco is out

Total drive time: ~20 minutes round trip from central Lincoln Park.

## Pumpkin Care Tips (For Us & Customers)

- **Put pumpkins ON the hay bales**, not directly on concrete — prevents bottom rot
- **Keep heirlooms out of direct sunlight** — lasts longer
- **Heirlooms last longer than regular pumpkins** — thicker skin, denser flesh
- **Watch out for squirrels and rats** — clear matte sealant or peppermint oil spray can help deter them
- **White pumpkins fade fast in sun** — place in shaded spots when possible
- **Shop early October** for best selection before stores run out of the good stuff
